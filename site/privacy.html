Privacy

Last updated September 27, 2026

The Gear Fox finds outdoor and running gear on sale in your size. We keep as little about you as we can, and we never sell it.

If you just browse

Your profile (sizes, categories, favourite brands) is saved in your own browser so the site remembers you next time. It isn't sent to us. We don't use tracking cookies or ads.

If you sign up for the Saturday email

  • What we keep: your email address, your profile, and when you confirmed and (if you do) unsubscribed. We keep the confirmation date as proof you asked for the email, as Canada's anti-spam law requires.
  • Why: only to send you the week's deals in your sizes and to let you open your profile from any device.
  • Where: in a database hosted by Supabase in Canada. Emails are sent through Resend, whose servers are in the United States.
  • Your private link: links in our emails contain a private key that opens your profile. Don't forward them if you'd rather others didn't see your sizes.

Links to stores

When you click a deal you go to the store's own site, and its privacy policy applies there. Some links may earn us a small commission at no cost to you; that never changes which deals we show.

Unsubscribe or delete your data

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your email and profile deleted completely, write to hello@thegearfox.com and we'll do it within 30 days.
