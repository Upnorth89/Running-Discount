Mystery shop: thegearfox.com

Oct 18, evening. I used the live site like a shopper on a phone (390px), a new women's size-8 visitor, then the same person coming back: welcome, sizes, deals, 11 searches, a deal click and the come-back card, hearts, menu, shoe pages, French, the Black Friday and size pages, and a computer screen.

Ranked by how much each one costs us. Fixed ones are already live or shipping tonight.

Already fixed tonight

  • Fixed

    A search with no results showed no message

    Anyone who hadn't signed up and searched for something we don't carry ("lululemon") saw "0 deals in your size today" and the sign-up pitch, nothing else. The robot missed it because its pretend visitor had already tapped "No thanks".

    Fix: no results now shows "Nothing matches right now…" and no sign-up card. New robot check added.

  • Fixed

    A store search put the sign-up card above the results

    Searching "altitude" showed "See every deal at Altitude Sports", then the big sign-up card, then the deals.

    Fix: the card now always comes after the first block of deals.

Worth fixing next

  • High

    Shoe pages show the men's table first to a women's shopper

    On the Pegasus 41 page, a women's size-8 visitor sees "Your size 8: $107.97" at the top, then a full Men's table, and has to scroll past it to find the Women's table. The price-history line under the Women's table talks about the men's price.

    Fix: open on the visitor's own table and show each table's own price line. It's a bug-level fix, small.

  • High

    A pop-up message covers the main button after a heart

    Tapping "Watch price" opens the email sheet and, at the same moment, a "Watching. Sign up for the Friday email…" message that sits right on top of the sheet's main button.

    Fix: no message when the sheet opens (the sheet already says it). Bug, I'll fix it with your OK on the batch.

  • High

    The first rows are crowded by one brand and by gym or lifestyle clothes

    "Dropped today" showed Sugoi in 5 of the first 7 spots. Top deals for a women's runner opened with an adidas cover-up jacket and "Optime Training 7/8 Leggings" (gym wear), and "sneakers" found Skechers BOBS casual shoes. Real running deals are there, but the first screen makes us look like a generic discount site.

    Fix: at most 2 per brand in the first rows; rank training and lifestyle lines lower; drop Skechers BOBS like other casual shoes. The brand cap and ranking are visible changes, so they'd be a test.

  • Medium

    Cards read "FuelCell SuperComp Trainer v3 Road Running Shoes - Women's" and NNormal's "Trail running shoes - Tomir 2.0 - BEIGE - Unisex". It's long and messy on a phone.

    Fix: trim "Road/Trail Running Shoes" from shoe names on cards, and clean NNormal's pattern at the source. Data fix.

  • Medium

    The welcome screen hides its third example deal

    The welcome card's third deal (Hoka Cielo RD) sits under the sticky "Find my deals" button. On a slow connection the three examples don't show at all until the data arrives.

    Fix: room for the third card above the button, or show two.

  • Medium

    "Just browse" puts its "pick your size" note in the wrong place

    The note "Showing every size. Pick your size and we'll hide what won't fit" sits under the swipe row, so it reads like part of the deals.

    Fix: move it right under the top line.

  • Medium

    Very long pages

    The homepage is 16 phone screens; the shoe list page is 20. Most people never reach the bottom, so the bottom categories (gloves, hats, gear) get little attention.

    Fix: no change for now. The 'layout' test and the category buttons already let people jump. Revisit with scroll-depth data from the funnel report.

  • Low

    French pages keep English product names

    Expected: names come from the stores. The interface itself is fully French.

What worked well

  • Sizes step: two taps (fit, size) and deals show.
  • Search handles real shopper words: "alphafly", "hoka clifton", "rain jacket", "trail shoes", and "kayano 31" explains it exists only in other sizes.
  • The "Didn't buy it?" card after a store visit is clear and asks one thing.
  • Shoe pages answer the question fast: "Your size 8: $107.97 at Sport Chek" right at the top.
  • Black Friday page: clear countdown, a sign-up box that asks only fit, size and email.
  • No script errors on any page.

One thing I did not count as a problem: my sandbox's connection made a page load look like 10 seconds once. The same page loaded in 4 seconds on the next run, and the data file (~1 MB compressed) should take about 2 to 3 seconds on a normal phone signal.
