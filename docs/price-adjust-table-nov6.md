- Sporting Life: LIKELY YES, 14-day price adjustment + price match (Google AI summary citing sportinglife.ca Help & FAQs: "To qualify for a price match or a 14-day price adjustment…"; online purchases: contact customer care within 14 days; competitor must be an authorized Canadian retailer with a physical store in the ship-to province, identical model/colour/size, new, in stock at both, CAD). Needs the store page's own text before it goes in a claim email.
- REI: Bastien says 14 days (to confirm with REI's own page text).
- MEC: Bastien says 30 days + price match (to confirm with MEC's own page text).
- Running Room: Bastien says 10 days + price match; our read of its own page: 10 days, but it excludes "sale-priced items… Black Friday… Boxing Day" and matches local physical competitors only. Ask the store whether a drop on its own site after an online purchase counts.
- Running Room, CONFIRMED NO for our use (Bastien's screenshot of ca.shop.runningroom.com/en_ca/price-matching, Nov 6): "Running Room offers price protection: Present your receipt with proof that the item is being advertised … within 10 days of purchase and we will refund the price difference." BUT "Price matching/ Price Protection does not apply to electronics, nutrition, sale-priced items, limited-time offers (i.e. grand opening sales/Flash sales), damaged goods, seconds, close-out prices, or sale prices, including Black Friday Sales, Cyber Monday, or Boxing Day Sales prices." Matches only "regularly advertised prices", local physical competitors (no websites). A drop to a SALE price never qualifies, and that is what our alerts catch -> treat as NO; claim email: "Running Room's price protection excludes sale prices."
- Sporting Life, CONFIRMED YES (Bastien's screenshot of sportinglife.ca/en-CA/help.html, Nov 6, "An item that I recently purchased went on sale. Can I receive a price adjustment?"): "If the price of an item you recently purchased has been reduced, we will happily perform a one-time sale adjustment at your request. For qualifying items purchased online, please contact the Customer Care team within 14 days of placing your order. The item must still be in stock and must be available in the same colour and size. In store purchases requiring a price adjustment need to be completed at one of our retail stores." Price match: authorized Canadian retailer with a physical store in the ship-to province, verifiable, CAD, in stock at both, same model/colour/size, new, no membership retailers (Costco, MEC). EXCLUSION (end of the answer): "This policy does not apply to advertising errors or misprints, demo products, coupons, special and/or limited-time savings events (i.e. Flash Sales, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Boxing Day, grand openings, closeouts)." Claim emails quote it; a drop during Black Friday/Cyber Monday/Boxing Day likely does not qualify: say so ("Sporting Life's policy excludes Black Friday sale prices; you can still ask").
- REI (US), CONFIRMED YES (Bastien's screenshot of rei.com/help Price Adjustments, Nov 6): "What if something I purchase goes down in price? We're happy to honor the lower price." Eligible: "The item was purchased at REI within the last 14 days and is the same product number, color, and size as the item on sale at REI." Not eligible: REI Outlet purchases; purchases more than 14 days ago; purchases excluded by a coupon's terms; purchases that violate the REI.com Terms of Use (incl. robots/scrapers used to buy or extract data). How: retail = any REI store; REI.com = request a price adjustment online (rei.com form). Refund to the original payment in 2-3 weeks, no email notification. Note: our REI data comes only from Bastien's saved pages (no robot reads REI); claim emails link REI's own form.
- MEC, PARTLY CONFIRMED (Bastien's screenshots of mec.ca/en/explore/price-protection, page title "MEC 30 Day Price Protection", Nov 6): the Price Match Conditions section: "Membership required: You must be an MEC member and have made the original purchase using your membership. Guest purchases are not eligible for price matching." Identical product (same brand, model, year, season, colour, size), new condition; sold and shipped by a Canadian-based retailer, CAD (incl. mandatory fees, excl. taxes), available for immediate purchase through a regular retail channel; retailers EXCLUDED: "Breathe Outdoors, eBay, The Last Hunt, Marché Alpin"; "MEC may also exclude certain deals or sales at its discretion"; advertised or ticketed, verifiable, cannot be combined with other discounts; resale intent / Terms of Use violations not eligible; does not apply to special orders, pricing errors, liquidation sales, close-out or final sale items.
